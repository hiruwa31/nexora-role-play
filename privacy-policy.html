Nexora Role Play — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 3, 2026

Nexora Role Play ("Nexora RP", "we", "our", or "the Bot") is a Discord application developed and operated by hiruxx_31 for the Nexora RP team.

1. About the Bot

Nexora Role Play is a Discord bot designed to provide information about the Nexora RP server, including whether the server is currently online or offline.

2. Information We Collect

  • Discord User ID
  • Discord username

We only use this information when necessary to operate and support the Bot.

3. How We Use Information

Information accessed through Discord may be used to identify users interacting with the Bot, provide and maintain Bot functionality, respond to user commands or interactions, and troubleshoot technical or security issues.

We do not sell personal information or use Discord data for advertising.

4. Data Storage

We do not intentionally store Discord User IDs or usernames longer than necessary to provide the Bot's functionality. If information is temporarily processed by the Bot or its hosting provider, it is handled only for operating, maintaining, and securing the Bot.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or publicly distribute user information. Information may only be shared with service providers when reasonably necessary to operate the Bot or when required by applicable law.

6. Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of information associated with their use of Nexora Role Play by contacting hiruxx_31 on Discord. Please do not send passwords, authentication tokens, or other sensitive credentials.

7. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by the Bot against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

8. Children's Privacy

Nexora Role Play is intended to comply with Discord's minimum age requirements and applicable laws. We do not knowingly collect information from users in violation of applicable age restrictions.

9. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Bot's functionality or data practices change. The latest version will be published at the URL associated with the Bot.
